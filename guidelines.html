Community Guidelines

Effective date: May 2026  ·  hello@barrowbound.app

Barrowbound is built around a love of reading — and the people who love to read. Our community is somewhere we want everyone to feel welcome, safe, and genuinely glad they showed up. These guidelines exist to keep it that way.

The short version: Be kind. Be honest. Talk about books. Don't be a troll.

What We Celebrate Here

  • Sharing what you're reading, loving, hating, or couldn't finish
  • Honest reviews — including the ones that are critical or funny or heartbroken
  • Thoughtful discussion about books, genres, authors, and ideas
  • Recommending reads to strangers who might love them
  • Respectful disagreement — not everyone has to love the same books
  • Supporting other readers, especially those just starting out
  • Authors sharing their work, seeking ARC readers, and connecting with the reading community — in the Authors & ARCs category

Authors & ARCs

If you're an author, Barrowbound is genuinely glad you're here — not just as a reader, but as a creator. We have a dedicated Authors & ARCs forum category where you're warmly welcome to share your books, announce upcoming releases, post cover reveals, seek ARC readers, and connect with the reading community.

What's welcome in Authors & ARCs:

  • New release and upcoming book announcements
  • ARC requests and review copy opportunities
  • Cover reveals and behind-the-scenes updates
  • Q&As with your readers
  • Anything bookish you'd like to share about your work

Please keep author promotion within the Authors & ARCs category — general forum areas are for reader discussion.

What's Not Okay

Harassment and bullying

Targeting, threatening, or repeatedly bothering another user. Disagreeing with someone's review is fine. Making their life difficult because of it is not.

Hateful content

Content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, nationality, or any other characteristic is not welcome here and will be removed. New Zealand's Harmful Digital Communications Act 2015 applies.

Content that harms children

Any content that sexualises, exploits, or endangers minors — in any form — will result in immediate account termination and referral to the relevant authorities. No exceptions.

AI-generated content

Reviews and forum posts should reflect your genuine reading experience. Posting AI-generated reviews as your own is not in the spirit of this community and may be removed.

Spam and self-promotion

Unsolicited promotion in general forum areas is not permitted. Authors are welcome in the dedicated Authors & ARCs category.

Misinformation

Deliberately spreading false information — about books, authors, or anything else — is not in the spirit of this community.

Spoilers without warnings

Use the spoiler tag. Always. Even if the book came out 30 years ago. Especially if the book came out 30 years ago.

Impersonation

Don't pretend to be another user, author, or member of the Barrowbound team.

Illegal content

This includes pirated books, links to illegal download sites, or any content that violates applicable law.

Reviews

Your reviews belong to you and we value honest ones — including negative ones. A review that says "I absolutely hated this" is valid. A review that attacks the author as a person rather than the book is not.

Review bombing — leaving negative reviews not based on your actual reading experience — is not permitted.

Book Clubs and Groups

Owners of book clubs are responsible for setting the tone of their group. Barrowbound reserves the right to remove clubs that violate these guidelines even if the violation happens within a private group.

Reporting

If you see something that violates these guidelines, please report it using the report button in the app or contact us at hello@barrowbound.app. We review every report.

What Happens When Guidelines Are Broken

Depending on severity:

  • Content removal with or without notice
  • A warning on your account
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent account termination
  • Referral to authorities where content is illegal

We aim to be consistent and fair. If you think a moderation decision was wrong, email hello@barrowbound.app and we'll take another look.

A Note on Tone

Reading is personal. A book that changed your life might leave someone else cold. That's completely fine. What matters is that we're all here because we care about reading — and we're able to treat each other with the decency that deserves.

Happy Reading. 📚
